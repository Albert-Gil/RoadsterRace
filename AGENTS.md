# Roadster Race

Build on the approved browser prototype and local multiplayer: two to four sequential drawing turns, a shared race, independent corner thrust/hop controls, and finishing order. Preserve solo play. Public GitHub Pages hosting for testing, manual hop and the authored automatic Santa Monica ramp are authorized; new hop shortcuts, leaderboards, accounts and promotion remain later work.

- Stack: TypeScript, Babylon.js, WebGL 2, Vite; DOM/CSS controls.
- Keep `src/core` independent of rendering, DOM, assets, and theme settings.
- Presentation settings live in `src/presentation/themes.ts`; assets have stable roles.
- Preserve timing through route compilation. All players/strokes use the same pace mapping.
- Simulation uses a 60 Hz fixed step. Visual quality and theme must not change results.
- Touch ownership lasts until release/cancel; never route solely through the primary pointer.
- Use the pinned pnpm toolchain and lockfile. Run `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test`, and `pnpm build` after meaningful changes. Run relevant browser tests for input/session/presentation changes.
- Document physical Safari verification as pending until a real device has been tested. Automated mobile emulation is supplemental evidence.
- Preserve existing planning documents. Never put secrets or generated dependencies in version control.
- Local preview and the public Albert-Gil/RoadsterRace GitHub Pages testing site are authorized. External posts/promotion and additional hosting services remain outside the milestone. Deploy only verified main builds of dist/; keep assets/Home beneath the configured base path. No service worker/offline caching during testing. Physical hosted Safari checks remain pending until performed on hardware.
- Circuits must include authored inner barriers or solid obstacle areas that prevent excessive corner cutting while preserving deliberate runoff. Visible obstacles and collision footprints must agree. Route compilation must reject solid crossings with vehicle clearance. Future hop mechanics must respect protected infield areas and ordered course progression; shortcuts require explicit track design and validation.

- Race distance is selectable from one to six laps. Reuse one compiled line/pace across laps; scope session bests by circuit ID/version and lap count.
- Driver names and selected paint are presentation preferences. Preserve them across themes and rematches, render names as text, and keep player numbers visible when names/paints repeat. The six paint definitions live centrally in themes.ts.
- Drift coaching pop-ups and post-race driving advice are intentionally omitted. Keep necessary lap, finish, contact and recovery feedback concise.

- Playable courses are registered in src/content/circuits.ts. Use the selected TrackDefinition for drawing, simulation, projection and timing; keep scenery selection outside src/core. Course changes must discard all plans, readiness and race state while preserving driver preferences and lap count, and must dispose old scene resources.

- Local multiplayer includes physical car-to-car contact in individual races and tournaments. Use a non-overlapping, barrier-clear starting grid. Resolve all cars together before crediting progress/checkpoints/finishes; overlap correction must not award distance. Recovering cars wait for an unoccupied safe position; finished cars leave active contact.
- Races with multiple physical cars, including Solo Race against CPU drivers, begin with three seconds of ghosting, measured by the fixed simulation clock. Disable only car-to-car contact/impulses/scrapes during that period; barriers and surface physics remain active. Pause freezes the remaining time; every new race, rematch and tournament round starts a fresh ghost period. Time Trial has one physical car and no starting contact-ghost period. The duration is centralized in CONTACT_RULES.
- Tournament rounds award 10/6/4/0 points. Hundredth-place ties share rank points and skip subsequent places; equal championship totals share the title. Commit each complete round exactly once. Preserve completed rounds across reloads, restart an interrupted round with fresh drawings, and validate saved circuit/rules/compiler versions.
- Player-facing venue names are Coastal Launch, Launchsite, Golden Gate and Santa Monica. Preserve the legacy orbital-launchyard identifier for preference/link compatibility; launchsite is a URL alias. Golden Gate is original stylized Bay Area scenery, with flat physical road and supported city blocks, a road-width suspension bridge and protected solid footprints.

- Drawing starts anywhere across the authoritative full-width start/finish gate, or within its 12 m approach. Preserve timestamped raw lead-ins/overshoots for display, but exclude them from geometry, pace, drawing duration and lap validation. Interpolate gate crossings. Every accepted lap connection must share a forward anchor, preserve pace and pass full vehicle-clearance validation before confirmation.
- The first valid forward finish crossing after a full lap ends the counted drawing. Permit release anywhere afterward; ignore all subsequent movement and pauses, with no overshoot-distance limit. Incomplete and wrong-way laps remain invalid.
- Phase-specific route widths live in themes.ts: planning 10/14 CSS px (color/outline), racing 8/11 px. Keep the planning vehicle clear of the full touch gate.
- Maximize the phone drawing surface: render scenery edge to edge and fit every interactive phase into the same safe course rectangle. Do not restore large header/footer bands, drawing instructions, pace legends or preview statistics. Use compact, accessible 44+ px controls (side rail in landscape, bottom rail in narrow portrait), keep numbered multiplayer identity and concise validation errors, and reserve explanatory copy/practice-line UI for a future tutorial. The practice fixture is development-only until that tutorial is explicitly built.
- Narrow bridge rails/supports are explicit, bridge-tagged exceptions to ordinary 5–7 m runoff. The deck is road-width only; visible rail geometry must exactly follow authoritative footprints. Do not reintroduce a shoulder/apron across water. City building footprints must be supported by authored land and protected areas; preserve a flat physical road.

- Keep repeated scenery colors and bounded cosmetic particles batched. Verify the 90 one-car Time Trial / 150 four-car (Solo Race or Multiplayer) draw-call targets with shadows, thrust and sand/impact effects active; cosmetic optimization must not alter simulation or input behavior.

- Santa Monica's automatic jump retains explicit supported land/deck footprints, water openings, forward ramp takeoff and approved flight/landing corridors. Geometry, route validation and visible supports must agree; airborne momentum/gravity and height-aware swept contacts run in the fixed simulation. No tire forces in flight; thrust remains available. Unauthorized water crossings and protected infield cuts remain invalid. Water failure returns to the last safe grounded pre-branch position without awarding progression/checkpoints, clears commands, and preserves time/recharge. Pier/gap rails are explicit runoff exceptions.
- Newly created Full Tours include all registered circuits. Existing saved schedules remain unchanged. Historic v7-to-v8 migration applies only to those unchanged versions; it is not permission to migrate championships to v10. Current handling uses rules v10; reject incompatible saves and preserve ordinary driver preferences.

- All four circuits use 16 m roads, including bridge/pier decks and the authored ramp, gap corridor and landing. Shared bounded road edges drive rendering and framing. Inside corners must not fold or pinch; validate full vehicle clearance, supported surfaces and protected runoff after layout changes.
- Use fixed whole-course framing in drawing, preview, countdown and racing. Fit actual road/gate/jump bounds with approximately 6 CSS px clearance inside the control/safe-area rectangle. Do not fit the broad sand apron or introduce automatic drawing/racing pan or zoom. Cancel a stroke before orientation reframing.
- Modes are Solo Race (one human plus three CPU drivers), Multiplayer (2–4 local humans), and Time Trial (one physical car). Human controls/drawing ownership are independent of car count. Solo and multiplayer support single races and tournaments; Time Trial ranks fastest laps.
- CPU drivers use validated independent plans, shared vehicle physics and legal ability commands on the fixed clock. Hop decisions require closing traffic and a clear supported straight/landing after starting ghosting ends. Difficulty changes planning/pace only, with slight opponent variation and no rubber banding. Save controller types, difficulty and deterministic seeds with championships. Rules/track-version changes invalidate old tours without erasing driver preferences.
- Time Trial ghosts are versioned cosmetic recordings of actual fixed-step motion. They never enter physics, contacts, occupancy, sound or standings. Save fastest-lap records/replays in IndexedDB, retain in-memory operation on storage failure, and freeze the chosen replay for each lap.

- Live thrust pads are 56 CSS px. Hide floating car/player tags during racing; retain player numbers and paints in controls, setup and results. Pause must offer an explicit Home action, preserving tournament exit/save safeguards.
- Starting grids must favor each driver's accepted gate anchor while remaining non-overlapping, road-supported and barrier-clear. Aim ahead through the gate rather than chasing the exact first route point; grid travel cannot award course progress. Preview and simulation must use the same grid allocation, including tournament rotations.
- The racing car artwork uses a centrally configured 1.12 visual scale for readability; keep its replay ghost and contact shadow consistent. The physical vehicle envelope remains 2.2 by 5.2 m. Verify visible barrier/contact readability after model changes.

- Manual Hop has an independent 7 s recharge and 4.9 m/s upward impulse (about 1 s airtime on level ground). One press triggers once; unavailable commands are discarded. It requires grounded support, is disabled in countdown/recovery/flight/finish, and never stacks a ramp lip impulse while airborne. Clear both ability commands on interruption; pause freezes timers.
- Flight assistance uses actual momentum and the nearby drawn line (manual) or approved landing corridor (ramp): at most 20 degrees at launch and 30 degrees/s in flight, without adding speed or rescuing reverse travel. Only airborne thrust accelerates horizontally; no tire forces or sand drag in flight. Sand drag is 0.95 with existing low grip unchanged.
- Hop does not authorize new shortcuts: above-road protected footprints and rails remain solid during flight. Submerged landing faces retain real vertical bounds so approved jumps pass above them. Water crossings still require the compiled authored jump and forward corridor; missing support recovers without progress. Contacts between cars remain height-aware.
- Retain 56 px thrust and 44 px hop controls inside the existing course rails, with separate recharge rings and player/ability pointer ownership. Preserve simultaneous independent touches, keyboard and assistive activation. Flight effects/audio are bounded presentation only; replay ghosts remain isolated.
