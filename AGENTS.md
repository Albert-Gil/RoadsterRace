# Roadster Race

Build on the approved browser prototype and local multiplayer: two to four sequential drawing turns, a shared race, independent corner thrust/hop controls, and finishing order. Preserve solo play. Public GitHub Pages hosting for testing, manual hop and the authored automatic Santa Monica ramp are authorized; the authored Launchsite manual-hop lane is authorized; the authored Grand Canyon natural-crest crossing and hidden manual-hop trail are authorized; LA nights and its authored channel jump and hidden backlot Hop lane are authorized. Open-terrain inside cuts are allowed; additional authored obstacle or water shortcuts, leaderboards, accounts and promotion remain later work.

- Stack: TypeScript, Babylon.js, WebGL 2, Vite; DOM/CSS controls.
- Keep `src/core` independent of rendering, DOM, assets, and theme settings.
- Presentation settings live in `src/presentation/themes.ts`; assets have stable roles.
- Preserve timing through route compilation. All players/strokes use the same pace mapping.
- Simulation uses a 60 Hz fixed step. Visual quality and theme must not change results.
- Touch ownership lasts until release/cancel; never route solely through the primary pointer.
- Use the pinned pnpm toolchain and lockfile. Run `pnpm typecheck`, `pnpm test`, and `pnpm build` after meaningful changes. Run relevant browser tests for input/session/presentation changes.
- After changing tuning constants or versions, run `pnpm docs:rules` (docs/CURRENT_RULES.md is generated and test-checked). Changes that must not alter race results (refactors, presentation) must pass `pnpm test:legacy` against HEAD.
- Document physical Safari verification as pending until a real device has been tested. Automated mobile emulation is supplemental evidence.
- Preserve existing planning documents. Never put secrets or generated dependencies in version control.
- Local preview and the public Albert-Gil/RoadsterRace GitHub Pages testing site are authorized. External posts/promotion and additional hosting services remain outside the milestone. Deploy only verified main builds of dist/; keep assets/Home beneath the configured base path. No service worker/offline caching during testing. Physical hosted Safari checks remain pending until performed on hardware.
- Circuits must include authored inner barriers or solid obstacle areas that prevent excessive corner cutting while preserving deliberate runoff. Visible obstacles and collision footprints must agree. Hop mechanics must respect protected infield areas and ordered course progression; crossings over protected obstacles or water require explicit track design (an authored jump or Hop lane). Open-terrain cuts may skip bends.

- Race distance is selectable from one to six laps. Reuse one compiled line/pace across laps; scope session bests by circuit ID/version and lap count.
- Driver names and selected paint are presentation preferences. Preserve them across themes and rematches, render names as text, and keep player numbers visible when names/paints repeat. The six paint definitions live centrally in themes.ts.
- Drift coaching pop-ups and post-race driving advice are intentionally omitted. Keep necessary lap, finish, contact and recovery feedback concise.

- Playable courses are registered in src/content/circuits.ts. Use the selected TrackDefinition for drawing, simulation, projection and timing; keep scenery selection outside src/core. Course changes must discard all plans, readiness and race state while preserving driver preferences and lap count, and must dispose old scene resources.

- Local multiplayer includes physical car-to-car contact in individual races and tournaments. Use a non-overlapping, barrier-clear starting grid. Resolve all cars together before crediting progress/checkpoints/finishes; overlap correction must not award distance. Recovering cars wait for an unoccupied safe position; finished cars leave active contact.
- Races with multiple physical cars, including Solo Race against CPU drivers, begin with three seconds of ghosting, measured by the fixed simulation clock. Disable only car-to-car contact/impulses/scrapes during that period; barriers and surface physics remain active. Pause freezes the remaining time; every new race, rematch and tournament round starts a fresh ghost period. Time Trial has one physical car and no starting contact-ghost period. The duration is centralized in CONTACT_RULES.
- Tournament rounds award 10/6/4/0 points. Hundredth-place ties share rank points and skip subsequent places; equal championship totals share the title. Commit each complete round exactly once. Preserve completed rounds across reloads, restart an interrupted round with fresh drawings, and validate saved circuit/rules/compiler versions.

- Accept lines as drawn (compiler v15, rules v14). A lap drawn from the gate in the circuit direction is accepted if it covers at least 72% of the circuit (centerline stations within 0.75 road widths of the asphalt, or spanned by an authored branch the line takes) and is one lap. Never reject a line for barriers, water, unsupported or off-trail terrain, distance from the road, a sloppy jump or a partial Hop lane; consecutive touch samples more than two road widths apart still mean lost contact. Off-road stretches are raced on sand/dirt with the normal grip and drag penalties.
- Stretches of the line the car cannot drive (vehicle capsule meets a barrier, or the car is off support outside an approved jump) become route hazards (src/core/hazards.ts). The car crashes or falls there; recovery at or within 12 m before a hazard places it just past the hazard, held for 1 s plus the skipped circuit distance / 20 m/s (RULES.hazardPenaltySpeed), so crashing through never pays. A second recovery within 15 m of the previous one also moves the car forward to the next clear, supported point, so no line can trap a car. Elsewhere recovery returns to the last safe position. Lines that were valid before have no hazards and race identically.
- Authored jumps are approved when the line enters the ramp or crest corridor and leaves beyond the landing inside it, moving forward; Hop lanes are approved when the line crosses the named obstacle moving forward with the whole car inside the lane. Unapproved attempts are accepted and simply get no exemption. The preview marks the first hazard and says how many there are; it never blocks racing.
- Off-road excursions far from the circuit are kept as drawn: loop and backward-hook cleanup ignores points more than one road width from the course.
