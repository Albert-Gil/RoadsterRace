Build on the approved browser prototype and local multiplayer: two to four sequential drawing turns, a shared race, independent corner thrust/hop controls, and finishing order. Preserve solo play. Public GitHub Pages hosting for testing, manual hop and the authored automatic Santa Monica ramp are authorized; the authored Launchsite manual-hop lane is authorized; the authored Grand Canyon natural-crest crossing and hidden manual-hop trail are authorized; LA nights and its authored channel jump and hidden backlot Hop lane are authorized; additional shortcuts, leaderboards, accounts and promotion remain later work.
